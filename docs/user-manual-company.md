# Company User Manual

## Login

Use the company account:

- Email: `company@aaharrakshak.dev`
- Password: `password`

The ASP.NET Core Company Area consumes Spring Boot APIs and does not duplicate backend rules.

## Notices

1. Open the Company dashboard.
2. View show-cause notices assigned to your company.
3. Open a notice to review the published report reference, reason and due date.
4. Submit a response with response text and document metadata.

Document binaries are stored outside MySQL. The platform records object key, file name, content type, size and checksum for tamper detection.

## Due Process

- A notice does not automatically prove guilt.
- Company responses are reviewed by authorized senior officials.
- Any warning, recall, suspension or cancellation is a simulated platform action for academic demonstration.
- No real government licence action is performed.

## Privacy

Companies cannot view citizen names, phone numbers, email addresses, full complaint details, private evidence internals or exact citizen GPS locations.
